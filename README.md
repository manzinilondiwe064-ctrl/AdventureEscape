Adventure Escape SA - Mobile App

Adventure Escape SA Mobile App is the next step in our journey. In Task 1 we only had a website, but now we have created a mobile application to accommodate everyone and make adventure accessible right from your phone.

The app features four simple screens: Home, Packages, Calculate and Contact. Users can explore seven amazing adventures including Mountain Hiking, River Rafting, Zip Line, Rock Climbing, Forest Camping, Desert Safari and Kayak Adventure. The app is designed to accommodate all ages, so whether you are young or old, there is something you will enjoy.

Our smart calculator automatically works out your total cost, applies a 10% discount for groups of five or more, and adds 15% VAT with a clear breakdown.

And we still live by our slogan, "Today is a good day to go camping".

This mobile app makes booking easier, faster and available to everyone, anywhere.
